# Advanced SEO Optimization Skill

## 1. Technical SEO Architecture
- **Title Tag**: Clear, keyword-rich format: `Brand | Value Proposition | Regional Context`.
- **Meta Description**: Engaging, 150-160 characters with strong call to action.
- **Canonical URLs**: Unambiguous canonical links preventing duplicate content penalties.
- **Multilingual Support**: `hreflang` tags for regional languages (ru, ky, en).

## 2. Structured Data (Schema.org JSON-LD)
Implement rich snippets:
- `MedicalWebPage` / `MedicalOrganization` / `SoftwareApplication`: Defines medical AI capabilities, clinical verified protocols, and Ministry of Health alignment.
- `FAQPage`: Rich snippet FAQ accordions in Google search results.

## 3. Social & Discovery Meta (OpenGraph & Twitter)
- `og:type`, `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`, `og:image:width`, `og:image:height`, `og:url`, `og:site_name`.
- `twitter:card` (summary_large_image), `twitter:title`, `twitter:description`, `twitter:image`.

## 4. Crawlability & Indexing
- `robots.txt`: Explicit rules for Googlebot, Yandex, Bingbot, protecting admin paths (`Disallow: /#admin`).
- `sitemap.xml`: Complete index of page sections and language alternates with lastmod and changefreq.
- Performance: `preconnect` and `dns-prefetch` for external CDN fonts and APIs.
